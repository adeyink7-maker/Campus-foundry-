TRANSFORM: getBoundingClientRect()

1. First (Initial Geometry)

Before launching, the button's exact viewport coordinates are measured via button.getBoundingClientRect().

2. Last & Invert (Target Geometry)

The container targets its destination dimensions in the viewport, and transforms backward to match the button's position and scale.

3. Play (Hardware-Accelerated Animation)

A single fluid transition smoothly animates the transform back to translate(0, 0) scale(1) with spring-like cubic bezier easing.
